Privacy Policy

Geoid Globe · Effective 26 October 2026

Geoid Globe is made by osivibecode. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on iPhone, iPad or Android.

In short

Geoid Globe does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps a few settings on your device so they are there next time you open it:

  • the places and time zones you save in My Places;
  • which saved clock you chose as Home.

This information stays on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Your device's own backup (iCloud or device backup on iOS, Google backup on Android) may include these settings, as it does for most apps. That backup is handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies, not by us.

What the app does not do

  • It does not use your location. Time zones and the globe come from data built into the app.
  • It does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or other personal data.
  • It does not contain advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, or any other third-party code that collects data.
  • It does not sell or share data, because it has none to share.

App stores

When you download the app, Apple's App Store or Google Play may collect information under their own privacy policies. We may see the anonymous, aggregated statistics they provide to developers, such as download counts. These never identify you.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a new feature needs data, we will update this page and its effective date before that version of the app is released.
