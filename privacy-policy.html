Privacy Policy

Geoid Globe · Effective 27 October 2026

Geoid Globe is made by osivibecode. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on iPhone, iPad or Android.

In short

Geoid Globe does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It sends an anonymous report when the app crashes, so we can fix the problem, and on iPhone and iPad it asks the App Store whether a newer version is available.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps a few settings on your device so they are there next time you open it:

  • the places and time zones you save in My Places;
  • which saved clock you chose as Home.

This information stays on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Your device's own backup (iCloud or device backup on iOS, Google backup on Android) may include these settings, as it does for most apps. That backup is handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies, not by us.

Crash reports

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service from Google, to tell us when it crashes. If the app crashes, it sends a report the next time you open it. A report contains:

  • technical details of the crash, such as which part of the app failed;
  • your device model, operating system version and the app version;
  • a random identifier for this installation of the app, so we can tell whether one crash happened once or many times.

Crash reports do not contain your name, email, location, saved places or anything else that identifies you, and we do not use them to track you or for advertising. We use them only to find and fix bugs.

Google processes crash reports for us and keeps them for 90 days. Their servers may be outside your country. Google's handling of this data is described in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Update check

On iPhone and iPad, the app asks Apple's public App Store lookup service which version of Geoid Globe is the latest, so it can tell you when an update is available. The request contains only the app's App Store ID. It sends nothing about you or your device, and Apple handles it under its own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • It does not use your location. Time zones and the globe come from data built into the app.
  • It does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or other personal data.
  • It does not contain advertising or analytics services. Crash reporting, described above, is the only third-party service in the app.
  • It does not sell your data or share it with anyone other than Google, which processes crash reports for us.

App stores

When you download the app, Apple's App Store or Google Play may collect information under their own privacy policies. We may see the anonymous, aggregated statistics they provide to developers, such as download counts. These never identify you.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Crash reports, described above, do not identify the person using the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a new feature needs data, we will update this page and its effective date before that version of the app is released.
